# 🥖 Pane Pugliese – Version 1  
### (Mixed Wheat Dough with Preferment)

## Ingredients

### **Preferment**
- 200 g bread flour (Type 550 equivalent)  
- 120 g water  
- 1 g fresh yeast  

### **Final Dough**
- All of the preferment  
- 200 g bread flour  
- 100 g durum wheat flour (Semola)  
- 240 g water  
- 15 g salt  
- 10 g good apple vinegar (or any light vinegar)  
- 4 g fresh yeast  

---

# Instructions

## **1. Preferment**
1. Dissolve the yeast in lukewarm water.  
2. Mix all ingredients thoroughly.  
3. The preferment will be very stiff — this is correct.  
4. Let it ferment at room temperature for 2 hours.  
5. Then refrigerate for 14–16 hours, covered.

---

## **2. Final Dough**

### **Autolyse**
1. Mix the flours and water.  
2. Cover and let rest for 30 minutes.

### **Finish the Dough**
1. Add the preferment.  
2. Add salt, yeast, and vinegar.

---

## **Kneading**

### **Thermomix**
- Knead for 75 seconds  
- Reverse mode  
- Speed 5  

### **Stand Mixer (KitchenAid, Kenwood, etc.)**
- Knead 5 minutes on the lowest speed  
- Then 10–20 minutes on speed 2–3  

**Important:**  
The dough must be fully developed → it should pull away from the bowl and pass the windowpane test.

---

## **3. Bulk Fermentation**
- Let rest 30–60 minutes.  
- Stretch and fold twice during this time.  
- The dough should increase in volume by at least **50%**.  
- If not, give it more time.

### **Cold Bulk Fermentation Option**
- After 15 minutes of rest, refrigerate the dough for 8–12 hours.  
- Reduce yeast to **2 g** if using this method.

---

## **4. Shaping & Final Proof**
1. Shape into a round loaf.  
2. Let rest another 30–60 minutes.  
3. Dough should again increase significantly in volume.

---

## **5. Baking**
- Score the loaf.  
- Bake for **40 minutes**, starting at **250 °C (480 °F)** and lowering to **200 °C (390 °F)**.  
- Let cool before slicing if you can.
