# Pizza with Stracciatella, Mortadella & Pistachio Pesto

## Shopping List

- 1 x Stracciatella (about 200 g)
- 1 x Mortadella Bologna (about 150 g)
- 1 jar Pesto di Pistacchio
- 1 x Mozzarella Fior di Latte (about 200 g)
- Granella di Pistacchio (chopped pistachios)
- Extra Virgin Olive Oil
- Black Pepper

## Preparation

1. Prepare and stretch the pizza dough.
2. Top with Fior di Latte.
3. Bake at 250–300°C for 7–10 minutes.
4. Add Stracciatella after baking.
5. Layer Mortadella on top.
6. Drizzle with Pistachio Pesto.
7. Sprinkle with chopped pistachios.
8. Finish with olive oil and black pepper.

Enjoy!
