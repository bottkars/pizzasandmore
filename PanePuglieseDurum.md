# 🥖 Pane Pugliese – Version 2  
### (100% Durum Wheat – Semola Rimacinata)

## Ingredients

### **Sourdough**
- 75 g Italian durum flour (Semola rimacinata)  
- 75 g water (about 40 °C / 104 °F)  
- 7 g sourdough starter  

### **Final Dough**
- All of the sourdough  
- 305 g Italian durum flour (Semola rimacinata)  
- 190 g water  
- 45 g additional water (optional, depending on dough softness)  
- 5 g yeast  
- 12 g salt  

---

# Instructions

## **1. Sourdough**
1. Mix flour, water, and starter.  
2. Ferment for 8–12 hours at room temperature.

---

## **2. Final Dough**

### **Autolyse**
1. Mix flour and **190 g water** until smooth.  
2. Cover and rest for 60 minutes.

---

## **3. Kneading**

### **Thermomix**
1. Add all remaining ingredients except the extra 45 g water.  
2. Knead 2 minutes, reverse mode, speed 5.  
3. Slowly drizzle in the remaining 45 g water.  
4. If the dough already feels very soft, skip the extra water.

### **Stand Mixer**
1. Add remaining ingredients (except the extra water).  
2. Knead on medium speed for 5–6 minutes.  
3. Increase to high speed until the dough releases from the bowl.  
4. Slowly add the remaining 45 g water.

**Important:**  
The dough must be fully developed → it should pull away from the bowl and pass the windowpane test.

---

## **4. Bulk Fermentation**
1. Place dough in a lightly oiled container.  
2. Ferment for 2 hours at about **25 °C (77 °F)**.  
3. Stretch and fold every 30 minutes.

---

## **5. Shaping & Final Proof**
1. Shape the dough.  
2. Place in a proofing basket.  
3. Proof for 1 hour at about **30 °C (86 °F)**.  
   - Cooler temperatures require longer proofing.

Preheat the oven to **250 °C (480 °F)** with a baking stone or — even better — a cast‑iron Dutch oven.

---

# 🔥 Baking

## **Dutch Oven Method**
1. Score the dough.  
2. Carefully place it into the hot Dutch oven.  
3. Bake **25 minutes with the lid on**.  
4. Remove lid, reduce temperature to **230 °C (445 °F)**.  
5. Bake another **15–20 minutes**.

---

## **Baking on a Stone**
1. Score the dough.  
2. Transfer to the hot baking stone.  
3. Steam the oven generously.  
4. Release steam after 10 minutes.  
5. Bake a total of **45 minutes**, reducing temperature from **250 °C to 220 °C** (480 °F → 430 °F).
