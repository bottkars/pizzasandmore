# 🍕 Quick Pizza Dough (EN)

## ⏱️ Overview
- **🕒 Preparation Time:** 8 minutes
- **⏳ Rising Time:** 50 minutes
- **🍽️ Course:** Main Dish
- **🇮🇹 Cuisine:** Italian
- **🍕 Yield:** 4 small pizzas

## 🛒 Ingredients
- 🌾 300 g flour (Type 00 or all-purpose flour)
- 💧 200 ml lukewarm water
- 🦠 4 g dry yeast (or 8 g fresh yeast)
- 🧂 5 g salt
- 🍬 ½ tsp sugar
- 🫒 1 tbsp olive oil

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

1. 💧 Pour the lukewarm water into a mixing bowl.
2. 🦠🍬 Add the yeast and sugar, then stir until the yeast is fully dissolved.
3. 🌾 Mix in the flour until a sticky dough forms.
4. 🧂 Add the salt and mix well, then incorporate the 🫒 olive oil.
5. 💪 Knead the dough vigorously for at least 5 minutes.
6. 🔪 Divide the dough into 4 equal portions.
7. ⚪ Shape each portion into a smooth dough ball.
8. 🥣 Place the dough balls separately and cover them airtight.
9. ⏳ Let them rise at room temperature for at least 20 minutes (longer is even better).

## 💡 Notes
- ✅ A longer rising time improves both flavor and texture.
- 🔥 Perfect for a quick homemade pizza.
- 🧀 Great with classic or creative toppings.
